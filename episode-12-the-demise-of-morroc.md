# Episode 12: The Demise of Morroc

BakbakanRO is centered on **Episode 12: The Demise of Morroc**, one of the defining eras of classic Ragnarok Online.

This episode represents the point in the world's progression where the threat surrounding Morroc reaches its climax and **Satan Morroc** becomes a major part of the story and high-level adventure.

## BakbakanRO Episode Scope

The server world is intentionally limited to content available up to Episode 12.

This means BakbakanRO is designed around the classic era before Third Jobs and later-generation progression systems became part of Ragnarok Online.

Players can expect progression built around:

- Classic first and second jobs
- Transcendent classes
- Classic Expanded classes
- Pre-Renewal mechanics
- Classic equipment progression
- Classic MVP hunting
- PvP and War of Emperium

## The Demise of Morroc

Morroc has long stood at the center of the desert region of Rune-Midgard. During the Episode 12 era, the city and surrounding region become central to the story involving the awakening and destruction caused by Satan Morroc.

The episode changes the atmosphere of the Morroc region and introduces high-level content associated with the aftermath of the disaster.

## Satan Morroc

Satan Morroc is one of the major threats associated with this episode and represents a significant challenge for experienced adventurers.

More detailed information about the related maps, monsters, quests, requirements, and rewards will be added to dedicated Episode 12 guides.

## Available Class Era

BakbakanRO keeps its class selection appropriate for the classic Episode 12 period.

### Regular Progression

Novice characters can advance through the classic first and second job paths and later rebirth into Transcendent classes.

### Expanded Progression

The following classic Expanded classes are available:

- Super Novice
- Taekwon Kid
- Soul Linker
- Taekwon Master
- Ninja
- Gunslinger

### Not Available

Third Jobs and later-era advanced class evolutions are not part of BakbakanRO's Episode 12 progression.

## Why Episode 12?

The goal is to preserve the feel of classic Ragnarok Online at the 99/70 stage while still providing a broad world for leveling, farming, MVP hunting, guild competition, PvP, and exploration.

Limiting the world to Episode 12 also keeps later classes, maps, equipment, and systems from changing the intended classic balance.
