# Getting Started

Welcome to BakbakanRO! This guide covers the basic steps you need before beginning your adventure.

## 1. Create Your Account

Create your game account through the official BakbakanRO website or account registration page.

Use a unique password and never share your login information with other players.

## 2. Download the Game

Download the latest official BakbakanRO client from the server's official download page.

For the best experience:

- Install the game in its own folder.
- Avoid placing the client inside another Ragnarok Online installation.
- Run the BakbakanRO patcher before starting the game.
- Allow the patcher to finish downloading all available updates.

Only use client files distributed or approved by BakbakanRO.

## 3. Patch Before Playing

Always launch the patcher before entering the game. This ensures that your client has the latest data files, interface updates, and server patches.

If you experience missing sprites, maps, items, or interface files, close the game and run the patcher again.

## 4. Log In and Create a Character

After the client is fully patched:

1. Start BakbakanRO.
2. Enter your registered account credentials.
3. Select the server.
4. Create your character.
5. Enter the world as a Novice.

Your first goal is to learn the basic controls and begin your first job progression.

## 5. Choose Your Class

BakbakanRO uses classic Ragnarok Online class progression.

You can follow the regular first-job and second-job paths, rebirth into a Transcendent class, or choose one of the available classic Expanded paths.

Expanded options include:

- Super Novice
- Taekwon Kid → Soul Linker / Taekwon Master
- Ninja
- Gunslinger

Third Jobs are not part of BakbakanRO's Episode 12 setting.

## 6. Learn the Server Settings

Before leveling, review the [Server Information](server-information.md) page. Important settings include:

- Pre-Renewal mechanics
- Maximum level 99/70
- Maximum stat 99
- Maximum ASPD 190
- Instant cast at 150 DEX
- x1 Base EXP, Job EXP, and Drop Rate

## 7. Explore Rune-Midgard

Once your character is ready, you can begin exploring towns, fields, and dungeons available within the server's Episode 12 world.

Your progression can include:

- Leveling and job advancement
- Farming equipment and cards
- Party play
- MVP hunting
- PvP
- War of Emperium
- Guild activities

## Need Help?

If you encounter a problem, check this wiki first and then use BakbakanRO's official community or support channels for assistance.

Continue with [Server Features](server-features.md) to learn more about the systems available in BakbakanRO.
